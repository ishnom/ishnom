## 👋 About Me

I'm a **Software Developer and Automation Engineer** who enjoys working across the full lifecycle of a project — from building APIs and web applications to automating repetitive workflows and turning raw data into useful insights.

My work sits at the intersection of **software development, automation, AI, and data analytics**.

I build backend systems, APIs, dashboards, automation pipelines, AI-powered tools, and data-driven applications. I enjoy taking a manual or complicated process and turning it into something **faster, smarter, and easier to use**.

* 📊 **Analyze** — Data, KPIs, trends and business performance
